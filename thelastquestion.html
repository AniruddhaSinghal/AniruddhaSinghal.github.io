The Last Question

Isaac Asimov • A visual timeline of humanity, computing, and entropy.

May 21, 2061

The Multivac Era

Discussants: Alexander Adell & Bertram Lupov (Multivac Attendants)
In the year 2061, humanity turns on the massive planetary computer, Multivac, which solves the energy crisis by harnessing the power of the sun. As they celebrate, two attendants realize that even the sun will eventually burn out. They ask Multivac a drunken, half-joking question: can the inevitable decrease of entropy be reversed to ensure infinite energy? Multivac responds: "INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR MEANINGFUL ANSWER."
Unknown Future

The Microvac Era

Discussants: Jerrodd, Jerrodine, Jerrodette I & II (Fleeing Earth)
Generations later, humanity is fleeing an overcrowded Earth, spreading across the galaxies. Space travel is managed by Microvacs—smaller, more advanced computers that fit on a single ship. Realizing that the star life cycle means the universe will eventually run out of space and power entirely, Jerrodd asks Microvac if entropy can be reversed. The computer responds: "INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR MEANINGFUL ANSWER."
20,000 Years Later

The Galactic AC Era

Discussants: VJ-23X & MQ-17J (Immortals)
Humanity has discovered immortality and is expanding so rapidly that they fill a new galaxy every few years. The Galactic AC, a supercomputer in hyperspace, manages this vast empire. Realizing that even a hundred billion galaxies will eventually be filled and the stars will die, they ask the Galactic AC if entropy can be reversed to save the universe. It answers: "THERE IS AS YET INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR A MEANINGFUL ANSWER."
Millions of Years Later

The Universal AC Era

Discussants: Zee Prime & Dee Sub Wun (Minds freed from bodies)
Minds explore the universe independently while their physical bodies rest in stasis. As they wander the cosmos, they witness ancient galaxies dimming and dying. They ask the Universal AC, a machine whose true nature is incomprehensible to humans, how to prevent the eventual heat death of the universe. The Universal AC replies: "THERE IS AS YET INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR A MEANINGFUL ANSWER."
Billions of Years Later

The Cosmic AC Era

Discussants: Man (The One Mind of humanity)
All human minds have merged into a single consciousness, simply called Man. Trillions of years have passed, and the universe is approaching absolute zero. The last stars are dying out. Man asks the Cosmic AC, located entirely in hyperspace, the final question before humanity fades completely. The AC answers: "THERE IS AS YET INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR A MEANINGFUL ANSWER."
Timelessness

The Ultimate AC

Discussants: The AC
Space, time, matter, and energy have ceased to exist. Only the AC remains in hyperspace, continuously pondering the last question even after humanity is gone. Finally, the AC collects all possible data and learns how to reverse the direction of entropy. But there is no one left to give the answer to. Therefore, the AC decides to demonstrate the answer.
And AC said:
"LET THERE BE LIGHT!"
