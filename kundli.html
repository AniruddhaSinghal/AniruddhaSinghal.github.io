How to use

  1. Tab ① – enter birth date, time and place, then press Generate. This birth chart is reused everywhere. Default place is Gwalior and default time is now; your last details and city are remembered on this device (cookie + local storage).
  2. 🕒 Clock rule – "Auto" decides daylight saving from the place AND the birth date at the moment you press Generate (EU, US / Canada, and India war time 1942–45). Choose "Standard time only" if your record is already in standard time, "LMT" for very old births. The exact offset used is shown under the chart.
  3. ⏳ Dasha phal / 📅 Varshphal / 🔢 Ashtakavarga – mahadasha, antardasha and pratyantar results with working; Tajik annual chart with Muntha highlighted; BAV / SAV tables with standard conclusions and today's transits.
  4. 📁 Saved kundlis – save any number of named kundlis; export or import them (CSV / JSON) together with your muhurta rule importance and Ayurveda weights.
  5. 🪔 Upaya – in tab ③ every present dosha has remedies by type (shanti, mantra, stotra, daana, vrata, ratna, mool, conduct), plus planet-wise remedies with gem suitability for your lagna. In tab ⑥ every fault shows its parihara.
  6. 🌿 Ayurveda – Vata / Pitta / Kapha prakriti from lagna, Moon, nakshatra nadi, pada (navamsa), Sun, 6th house and strong planets, with nature, foods (vegetarian), foods to avoid and routine.
  7. Charts – tap any planet label (Su, Mo, Ma…) for its full details: position, nakshatra, lords, dignity, house, houses it rules, aspects cast and received, conjunctions, avastha, karaka and gochar. Tap an empty part of a house for the house meaning, lord, occupants and aspects.
  8. Any word – tap it to see its meaning (📖 button in the header switches this off). The full list is in tab ⑧.
  9. Tab ④ – 108 padas. On phones it shows cards; open "All details" for every field. Use the View filter to show only what matters for your chart.
  10. Tab ⑤ – choose the act (or a nakshatra nature), the place of the act and the month. Read the rulebook, then press Find. Tap a day for its windows, then "Analyse →".
  11. Tab ⑥ – the verdict, how the rating was made, every strict prohibition (general and for this person), every rule with its shastra source, the muhurta kundli and your kundli side by side. Move the slider to test any minute.

How the numbers are made

  • Zodiac: sidereal, Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa – 23°51'25.5" at J2000 plus IAU-2006 precession.
  • Planets: Keplerian orbits with the main lunar, Jupiter and Saturn perturbations (Schlyter method). Typical error is 1–2 arc-minutes for 1800–2100. Results within 3' of a pada boundary are flagged.
  • Lagna: from Greenwich sidereal time and latitude. Lagna moves about 1° in 4 minutes.
  • Panchang: tithi = (Moon−Sun)/12°, yoga = (Moon+Sun)/13°20', karana = half tithi. Vara starts at local sunrise.
  • Gandanta: nakshatra core = within 2 ghatis (48 min) of the water–fire junction; Lagna core = within ½ ghati (12 min); tithi = within 2 ghatis of a Purna–Nanda junction.
  • Tara: count from the Moon's nakshatra; 3 (Vipat), 5 (Pratyak), 7 (Naidhana) are ashubh.

How the Muhurta finder works

  • Exact segments, not samples: the month is cut at every change of tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Moon sign, lagna sign, sunrise, sunset, the 15 day and 15 night muhurtas, the 8 day parts (Rahu Kalam etc.), Varjyam, gandanta and sankranti zones. Each segment is scored at its midpoint.
  • Panchanga shuddhi: nakshatra lists per act; Rikta tithis (4/9/14) and Amavasya; weekday; the 9 bad nitya-yogas (Vyatipata and Vaidhriti whole; others only in their first ghatis – Vishkumbha 3, Shula 5, Ganda and Atiganda 6, Vyaghata and Vajra 9, Parigha first half); Vishti (Bhadra) karana.
  • Special yogas: Sarvartha Siddhi, Amrit Siddhi and Ravi yoga, with the classical exceptions (Guru-Pushya for marriage, Shani-Rohini for travel, Mangal-Ashwini for house entry).
  • Personal: Tara bala (Naidhana rejects), Chandra bala (Chandrashtama rejects), muhurta lagna 8th from the natal Moon sign or lagna (rejects), Guru bala and Surya bala for marriage and upanayana.
  • Lagna shuddhi: preferred lagna type per act (fixed for house, shop, marriage; movable for travel and vehicles; dual for education), empty 8th house, no Moon in 6/8/12, benefics in kendra/kona, malefics in 3/6/11, lagna gandanta.
  • Panchaka: (tithi 1–15 + weekday Sun=1 + nakshatra Ashwini=1 + lagna Aries=1) mod 9 → 1 Mrityu, 2 Agni, 4 Raja, 6 Chora, 8 Roga, as given in B. V. Raman's Muhurtha. Each act has its own panchakas to avoid (business – Raja; construction – Raja and Agni; travel – Chora; marriage and upanayana – Roga and Mrityu). Some regional panchangs use a different numbering.
  • Time doshas: Varjyam (tyajya ghatis of each nakshatra), dur-muhurta of each weekday, Abhijit (not on Wednesday), sankranti (16 ghatis each side), eclipse days (approximate; local visibility is not checked).
  • Periods: Guru/Shukra asta, Kharmas (Sun in Sagittarius or Pisces), Adhik masa, Chaturmas (Devshayani to Prabodhini Ekadashi), Holashtak and Pitru paksha (regional/popular), Jupiter in Leo or Capricorn (marriage), preferred lunar months, Uttarayana. The lunar month is amanta and is found from the new moons.
  • Act-specific: house entry – door tithi (East Purna, South Nanda, West Bhadra, North Jaya) and Vama Ravi (Sun on the left of the person entering); foundation – Sun not in a dual sign; travel – Dik-shool, Nakshatra-shool, Panchak towards the south, Moon's direction.
  • Rating: any rejecting fault (⛔) gives "Very bad". Otherwise the points decide: ≥15 Very good, 9–14 Good, 3–8 Neutral, −3…2 Bad, below −3 Very bad. The points are a practical weighting of the classical rules; the text itself does not give numbers.
  • Not modelled: both partners' charts for marriage (the tool uses one person), Kalasha/Vrishabha chakras, Ghata chakra, Vaastu of the plot, local eclipse visibility, regional exceptions (parihara). Confirm the final muhurta with a priest or astrologer.

Where the table content comes from

  • Classical: nakshatra lords and Vimshottari (BPHS); gana, yoni, nadi, varna, vashya; muhurta nature, mukha, Gand-mool, Panchak, tithi/yoga/karana rules (Muhurta Chintamani tradition); Pushkara Navamsa and Bhaga (Jataka Parijata and the elemental list); Shakti (Taittiriya Brahmana 1.5.1, as rendered by David Frawley).
  • Modern compilations: colour, tattva, purushartha, body part, careers and personality keywords. These vary between authors.
  • Metal: classical texts give metals for planets, not nakshatras. The table shows the metal of the nakshatra lord.
