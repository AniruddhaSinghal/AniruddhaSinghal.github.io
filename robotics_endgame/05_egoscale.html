EgoScale

The Law of Robot Learning: Mathematical proof that physical dexterity scales predictably with volume of human-centric video data.

Hover over a data point on the graph to view the scaling milestones.
Basic Grasping
~1,000 Hours | Success: 12%
The model can perform simple pick-and-place tasks but is brittle. If lighting changes, or the object is rotated slightly, the robot fails completely. The internal "World Model" is barely formed.
Robust Perception
~10,000 Hours | Success: 31%
The robot stops failing due to visual distractors. It understands "what" an object is regardless of lighting or background, but still struggles with dynamic, multi-step physical interactions (like pouring liquid).
Zero-Shot Generalization
~100,000 Hours | Success: 70%
The critical tipping point. The model begins to understand physics and object affordances. It can successfully manipulate objects it has never explicitly seen in training because it understands the generalized rules of the physical world.
Emergent Dexterity
~1,000,000+ Hours | Success: 90%+
Human-level and superhuman dexterity. The model exhibits dynamic recovery (catching a slipping object), tool use without explicit programming, and complex bimanual coordination. This is the "ChatGPT moment" for robotics.
