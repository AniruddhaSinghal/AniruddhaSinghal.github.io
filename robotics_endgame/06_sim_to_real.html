Real-to-Sim-to-Real

The Digital Cousin: Scaling physical experience by scanning real objects, proceduralizing them in simulation, and transferring the mastery back to reality.

Step 1: Physical Space

Object Scanning

A simple smartphone camera captures multi-view video of a real-world object (e.g., a screwdriver on a desk).

Tech: Gaussian Splatting / NeRF
Step 2: Digital Space

3D Reconstruction

The video is converted into a highly accurate 3D mesh with embedded physical properties (friction, weight distribution).

Tech: Automated Mesh Generation
Step 3: Digital Space

The "Digital Cousins"

The single object is procedurally multiplied into thousands of variations (different lighting, colors, sizes, and camera angles) to prevent overfitting.

Tech: Domain Randomization
Step 4: Real Transfer

Zero-Shot Mastery

The robot agent practices interacting with the 1,000 digital cousins in GPU-accelerated simulation. When deployed to the physical robot, it handles the real screwdriver perfectly on the first try.

Tech: Sim-to-Real Transfer RL
