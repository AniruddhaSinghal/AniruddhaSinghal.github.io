The 2040 Ascent

The exponential trajectory of intelligence: From recognizing basic pixels to mastering the physical universe.

Hover over the timeline milestones to view the exponential leaps in AI capability.
2012
The Deep Learning Boom
Milestone: AlexNet
Compute: ~0.0001 PetaFLOP/s-days
Capability: Pixel Recognition

The catalyst. Using GPUs to train neural networks allows computers to accurately recognize cats and dogs in images. It is purely digital and entirely passive.

2020
Language Mastery
Milestone: GPT-3
Compute: ~3,640 PetaFLOP/s-days
Capability: Text Generation

The scale era begins. By predicting the next token across massive text datasets, models develop an emergent understanding of human grammar, logic, and facts. Still confined to digital text boxes.

2026
The Embodiment Era
Milestone: Agentic AI & General W.A.M.
Data: 1M+ Hours of Human Video
Capability: Digital & Physical Action

The knee of the curve. Intelligence transitions from thinking to doing. Agents navigate websites autonomously, and bipedal robots learn to walk and manipulate objects using Vision-Language-Action models.

2040
Physical A.G.I.
Milestone: The Singularity Node
Scale: Billions of deployed agents
Capability: Limitless Physical Agency

The End Game. The physical world becomes a fully programmable API. Robots possess superhuman dexterity, dynamically updating their physical world models in real-time, and building the infrastructure for civilization.
