The "Head-Heavy" Bottleneck Hover for stats
  • Architecture: Takes a massive LLM (e.g., PaLM-E, RT-2) and simply bolts on a vision encoder and an action output head.
  • Latency: Operates at roughly 1-5 Hz (too slow to catch a falling object or react dynamically).
  • Inefficiency: Wastes vast compute cycles reasoning through text space just to move a motor joint 2 degrees.
  • Result: Smart, but slow and clumsy in real-time.

Embodied Equilibrium Hover for stats
  • Architecture: Natively multimodal from the ground up. Vision, Language, and Action are processed jointly in the same latent space.
  • Latency: Operates at 50-100+ Hz, enabling fluid, human-like reaction times and dynamic recovery.
  • Efficiency: Pixel-to-torque predictions bypass the text bottleneck, allowing direct spatial and physical reasoning.
  • Result: Fast, dexterous, and fully aware.
