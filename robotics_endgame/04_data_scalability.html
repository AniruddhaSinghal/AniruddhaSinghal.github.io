Teleoperation

Scale: ~3 hours / day

Using VR headsets and custom rigs (like ALOHA) to manually pilot the robot. This gives the model explicit motor-torque data for specific tasks.

Trade-off: Highest quality, but lowest quantity. Impossible to scale to the millions of hours needed for generalized intelligence.

Data Wearables

Scale: 100,000+ hours

Humans wearing haptic gloves or exoskeletons while doing their normal jobs. This captures human-scale physical dexterity and force-feedback.

Trade-off: Medium quality, high quantity. Requires translating human hand kinematics (5 fingers) into robot gripper logic (2 fingers).

Egocentric Video

Scale: 10,000,000+ hours

First-person video from Smart Glasses, GoPros, and YouTube. Models learn physics, object permanence, and task sequencing just by watching.

Trade-off: Lowest specific motor control, but massive, internet-scale quantity. The foundation of general World Action Models.
